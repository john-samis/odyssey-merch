About

Odyssey Dance Troupe

For 30 years, the Odyssey Dance Troupe has been the Hellenic Community of Ottawa’s premiere performing group. Their performances are based on authentic and traditional dances from various regions of Greece and are truly a sight to see.

Over the years, the group has been a headlining performance at Ottawa GreekFest, and has attended various multicultural events in the National Capital Region.
